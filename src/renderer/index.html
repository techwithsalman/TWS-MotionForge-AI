About TWS MotionForge AI

Official desktop software information, branding, and licensing.

TWS MotionForge AI

Version 1.0.0 (Windows 10/11 64-bit)

Brand: Tech With Salman

TWS MotionForge AI is a professional, 100% FREE and offline-capable AI stickman video creation studio designed to democratize high-converting video production.

Key Commitments

  • 100% Free: No subscriptions, no usage billing, no paid cloud APIs.
  • Local-First: All speech synthesis, word alignment, animation rendering, and FFmpeg composition run locally on your PC.
  • Hardware Friendly: Runs smoothly on standard CPU hardware with zero mandatory discrete GPU requirement.
